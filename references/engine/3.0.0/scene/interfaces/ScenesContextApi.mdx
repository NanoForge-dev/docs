### load()

> **load**\<`TParams`\>(`ctx`, `scene`, `options?`): `Promise`\<`void`\>

Defined in: [modules/scene/src/scenes-context.type.ts:30](https://github.com/NanoForge-dev/Engine/blob/ed8c7e0b33d17f8343c32659cf63ba4166a4fa53/modules/scene/src/scenes-context.type.ts#L30)

Moves to a scene: unloads the loaded scenes up to the deepest one it
shares with `scene`'s parents, then loads its missing parents and the
scene itself.

#### Type Parameters

##### TParams

`TParams`

#### Parameters

##### ctx

[`Context`](/references/engine/3.0.0/ecs/index/interfaces/Context)

Handed to the `load` and `unload` of the scenes.

##### scene

[`SceneClass`](/references/engine/3.0.0/scene/interfaces/SceneClass)\<`TParams`\>

The scene to move to.

##### options?

[`LoadOptions`](/references/engine/3.0.0/scene/interfaces/LoadOptions)\<`TParams`\>

Its params, and the parent to load it under.

#### Returns

`Promise`\<`void`\>

A promise resolved once the change is made.

#### Remarks

The change is queued and made when no system runs (see `SceneLibrary`).
A scene already loaded is not loaded again: the scenes under it are
unloaded.

#### Throws

`NfSceneCycleException` (through the promise) When a scene ends up among its own parents.
